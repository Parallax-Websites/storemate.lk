The Return Problem

What costs you 20% of your revenue

Returns don't happen randomly. They happen for specific, identifiable reasons — and most can be stopped before the order ever ships.

20%
1 in 5 orders returns. Most aren't quality issues — they're operational failures. Duplicates. Fakes. Missed pending orders. Wrong picks. Every one is preventable.

Duplicate Orders

Customer orders the same item from Facebook, Instagram, and Daraz simultaneously. You ship three times. They return two. Margin destroyed.

Fake Customers

Problem buyers place orders, receive products, and immediately request returns. Or claim non-delivery. Your return rate spirals from the same few people.

Wrong Items Shipped

Staff misread orders. Wrong size. Wrong colour. Wrong product entirely. Customer receives the wrong item. Automatic return. Lost margin. Lost time.

Pending Orders Missed

Customer places a new order while their previous shipment is still in transit. You don't notice. You ship again. Now they have duplicates — and return one.

Return Processing Costs

Each return costs time, shipping, restocking, quality check. A 20% return rate means 20% of your revenue spent processing returns instead of generating profit.

Reputation Damage

High returns signal poor quality to marketplace algorithms. Your listing drops. Customers buy from competitors. One bad reputation is very hard to reverse.
