How it works

Three steps.
Orders appear in Storemate.

No integrations. No developer. No copy-paste. Just share your link and watch orders come in.

01
You share the link

Storemate generates your unique Self-Order Form link. Post it in your WhatsApp status, Facebook bio, Instagram story, or send it directly to customers. One link, anywhere.

02
Customer places the order

Customer opens the link on their phone. They browse your product catalogue, select items, set quantity, enter their name, number, and delivery address, then hit Place Order.

03
Order arrives in Storemate

The order lands in your Storemate dashboard instantly, complete with customer name, number, address, products, quantities, and COD value. Ready to process. Zero typing from your side.
