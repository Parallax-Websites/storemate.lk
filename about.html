Our story

We did not build software
and then try to understand sellers.

Storemate was built by the same team that operated Curfox, Sri Lanka's most widely used courier management platform for over five years.

We processed 25 million orders. We ran operations through 120 courier branches and 1,000 delivery riders. We worked directly with 30,000+ Sri Lankan sellers every single day.

We were not observers of Sri Lankan e-commerce. We were inside it, every order, every failure point, every moment a seller needed help and did not get it.

That experience is permanently ours. No competitor can go back in time and earn it.

How we got here

A journey built on
real experience.

2019

Curfox launched

Sri Lanka's courier management platform goes live. We begin processing orders for the island's growing e-commerce market.

2021

25M orders milestone

Curfox crosses 25 million orders processed, serving 30,000+ sellers across all major Sri Lankan courier networks.

2022

NBQSA, ICT Entrepreneur of the Year

First national recognition. The quality of our platform and our commitment to Sri Lankan sellers is formally acknowledged.

2023

APICTA Merit Winner · Storemate OMS launched

International recognition at Asia Pacific ICT Alliance Awards in Hong Kong. Storemate OMS launches, built on everything we learned from Curfox.

2024

The only platform recognised three years running. ISO 27001 certification process begins. Storemate grows to thousands of active sellers.

What we stand for

Six values.
All of them visible in how we operate.

Seller-first

Every decision starts with one question: does this make life easier for the seller? If not, we do not ship it.

Radical honesty

We say what we mean. We tell you what your data says, even when it is not what you wanted to hear.

Operational obsession

We are not a software company that thinks about operations. We are an operations company that builds software.

Human support

When a seller has a problem, a person responds. Not a bot. Not a ticket queue. A person who understands the business.

Data responsibility

Consistent excellence
