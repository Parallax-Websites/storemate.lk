Why we built this

We did not build this
from a meeting room.

Storemate comes from Curfox, Sri Lanka's leading courier aggregator. Before we wrote a single line of code, we spent five years processing millions of real orders alongside Sri Lankan sellers.

We watched sellers drown in notebooks, juggle three courier portals, and lose orders in chat threads. When they asked us why it had to be this hard, we stopped asking why and built the answer.
