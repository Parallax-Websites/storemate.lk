As your online business grows, you can manage more of it from one place. Start with the part you need most today. Everything stays connected in Storemate ERP.

All your online orders. One clear workflow.

Whether the order comes from Facebook, WhatsApp or TikTok, Storemate OMS keeps it in one place.

From confirming the order to printing the waybill, sending it to the courier and handling returns, your team follows one simple process.

  • Orders from Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok and your website in one list
  • One click sends the order to Royal Express, TransEx, FDE, DomEx or Koombiyo
  • Branded waybills printed in seconds, not written by hand
  • Duplicate and fake order checks before you ship
  • Returns handled against the original order
  • Full customer and delivery history on every order

From counter sale to stock update. One system.

Storemate POS makes everyday billing faster while keeping your inventory, customers, payments and outlets connected.

Every sale updates your business records automatically, so your team always works with the latest information.

  • Barcode scanning with instant product search
  • Automatic pricing, discounts and taxes on every line
  • Cash, card and split payments at checkout
  • Batches, expiry, variants and units
  • Multiple outlets with stock transfers between them
  • Roles and permissions per cashier and per outlet

Storemate E-Commerce connects your website or Shopify store with Storemate, so orders, products and stock stay updated in real time.

When a customer places an order online, it comes directly into Storemate, while stock updates automatically across both systems.
