**THE PREMIUM HERO — HOW TO MAKE IT FELT**

The feeling of becoming a professional Entrepreneur cannot be stated. It has to be **experienced in the first 3 seconds of landing on the page.**

Here is how every dimension of the hero must work together to create that feeling.

---

## The Core Principle

> **The hero must make the seller feel they have already arrived — before they read a single word.**

The visual, the typography, the animation, the language — all of it must say: *"This is what serious looks like. And you are here."*

---

## 01 — The Visual Identity of the Hero

**Background — do not use a flat gradient.**

A flat gradient reads as standard SaaS. Premium reads as depth and atmosphere.

```
LAYER 01    Deep navy #013387 — full canvas
LAYER 02    A soft radial glow in #006DAF
            positioned centre-right — like a
            light source behind the content
LAYER 03    Very subtle grain texture — 4% opacity
            This is the detail nobody notices
            consciously but everybody feels
LAYER 04    A faint horizontal line grid or
            topographic pattern — 3% opacity
            Adds depth without decoration
LAYER 05    A 3px cyan line at the very top —
            the brand signature in every dark section
```

This combination feels like a stage was set for the seller. Not a landing page. An arrival point.

---

## 02 — The Typography — Weight Contrast Is Everything

The tagline pairing must feel like a statement and a destination simultaneously.

```
LINE 01     "From seller to"
            Forefront BookSH · Weight 300 · Light
            Large · White · Generous letter-spacing
            This line is the before.
            It acknowledges where they are now.

LINE 02     "Entrepreneur."
            Forefront BookSH · Weight 900 · Bold
            Larger than line 01 by 4 to 8px
            White or very subtle cyan tint
            This line is the after.
            It names where they are going.
```

The size difference between the two lines is critical. Line 02 must feel heavier and more significant. The visual weight of "Entrepreneur." must land like a statement — not just a word.

```
LETTER SPACING      -1px to -2px on both lines
                    Tight tracking signals premium
                    Loose tracking signals startup

LINE HEIGHT         1.1 to 1.15
                    Tight. Confident. Not airy.

MARGIN BETWEEN      8px only
                    The two lines belong together.
                    They are one thought.
```

---

## 03 — The Supporting Line — Proof Before Promise

Most hero supporting lines describe the product. This one must validate the decision the seller is about to make.

```
CURRENT
"Built from the experience of 25M orders
and 30,000+ Sri Lankan sellers."

THIS IS CORRECT.

It tells the seller:
"The people behind this platform have
already walked the path you are on.
They know it. They built for it."

That feeling — of being in experienced hands —
is the foundation of the premium feeling.
```

**Typography for the supporting line:**

```
Font        Forefront BookSH · Weight 300
Size        17px to 18px
Color       White at 60% opacity
            Not full white — that competes with
            the headline. Softer. Secondary.
Max-width   520px centred
Line-height 1.7 — more generous than the headline
```

---

## 04 — The Eyebrow — Small Detail. Big Signal.

The tiny line above the headline is doing important psychological work.

```
TEXT        THE STANDARD FOR SRI LANKAN E-COMMERCE

OR          STOREMATE · WAVE DESIGN SYSTEM

Font        Weight 700
Size        10px to 11px
Color       Cyan #00BCE7 — 70% opacity
Spacing     Letter-spacing 0.2em — wide
Transform   UPPERCASE
Margin      20px below eyebrow · 0px above headline
```

This line tells the seller before the headline: *"You are about to interact with something that has been recognised as the standard."* It frames the headline with authority before it is read.

---

## 05 — The CTA — Premium Buttons Do Not Beg

Most CTAs shout. Premium CTAs state.

```
PRIMARY BUTTON
Text        "Start free for 3 months"
            Not "Sign up now" — that sounds transactional
            Not "Get started" — that sounds generic
            "Start free for 3 months" — specific,
            low-risk, confidence-signalling

Background  Cyan #00BCE7
Text color  Navy #013387 — dark on light
Font        Weight 800 — the boldest element below
            the headline
Size        15px
Padding     14px top/bottom · 32px left/right
Radius      8px — not pill, not sharp
Hover       Slight lift — translateY(-2px)
            Opacity 0.92
            Transition — 280ms

SECONDARY BUTTON
Text        "See how it works"
Style       Ghost — transparent background
            White border at 35% opacity
            White text at 90% opacity
Hover       Border opacity increases to 70%
```

**Rule:** Two buttons maximum. Primary is cyan. Secondary is ghost. No other options. Choice beyond two signals uncertainty.

---

## 06 — The Reassurance Line — Remove the Last Fear

Directly below the buttons. Small. Essential.

```
TEXT        No card needed · Full setup by our team · Cancel anytime

Font        12px · Weight 400
Color       White at 35% opacity
Spacing     14px margin top from buttons

PURPOSE     This line removes the three biggest fears
            a Sri Lankan seller has before signing up.

            "No card needed"     — I will not be charged
            "Full setup by our team" — I will not struggle alone
            "Cancel anytime"     — I am not locked in
```

These three phrases are worth more than three paragraphs of feature description.

---

## 07 — The Animation — Premium Moves Differently

Generic hero animations fade everything in at once. Premium animations have rhythm and intention.

```
SEQUENCE — staggered reveals

0.0s    Nothing visible except the background
        The stage is set before the performer arrives

0.1s    Background glow pulses in softly
        The atmosphere establishes itself

0.3s    Eyebrow fades up — 0.4s duration
        The authority line arrives first

0.5s    Line 01 "From seller to" slides up — 0.5s
        The acknowledgment of where they are

0.7s    Line 02 "Entrepreneur." slides up — 0.5s
        Slightly faster than line 01
        More weight means more decisiveness

0.9s    Supporting line fades in — 0.4s
        The proof settles beneath the promise

1.1s    CTA buttons appear — 0.3s
        The action is the last thing to arrive
        Because the trust must be built first

1.3s    Reassurance line fades in — 0.3s
        The final comfort, at the very end
```

This sequence takes 1.6 seconds total. The seller reads while it plays. By the time the CTA appears — the emotional journey is complete.

---

## 08 — The Composition — Where Things Live

The composition itself signals premium or standard.

```
CENTRED LAYOUT — for maximum impact

All content centred on the canvas.
Maximum width 640px for text content.
Generous vertical spacing between elements.

TOP         3px cyan accent line — the brand signature
            Eyebrow — 28px below the line
HEADLINE    32px below the eyebrow
SUPPORTING  20px below the headline
BUTTONS     36px below the supporting line
            Centred · Inline · Gap 12px between
REASSURANCE 14px below the buttons
```

**The white space rule:** The hero should feel like there is too much space. That discomfort you feel looking at a premium hero — that is the premium signal working correctly. Fill the space and it becomes a startup page immediately.

---

## 09 — The One Visual Element That Elevates Everything

Add one element behind the text that is not part of the navigation, headline, or CTA:

```
OPTION A — The subtle wave pattern
A very faint representation of the wave mark
from the logo — expanded as a background
texture. 3% opacity. The brand mark becomes
the atmosphere.

OPTION B — The particle field
Tiny dots of white at random positions.
0 to 1 opacity. Very slow drift.
Like stars — or the millions of orders
the platform was built from.
Subtle. Never distracting.

OPTION C — The light beam
A single wide gradient column from top-centre
to bottom — like a spotlight on the content.
Navy edges. Slightly lighter centre.
Focuses the eye without announcing itself.
```

**Recommendation: Option B** — The particle field. It connects subconsciously to the brand story. 25 million orders. Thousands of sellers. Countless moments. All represented as a quiet, breathing background that makes the hero feel alive without moving anything the eye catches directly.

---

## 10 — The Language — Last and Most Important

Everything above is form. This is substance.

The headline already works. The supporting line already works. The one addition that elevates the premium feeling further:

**Add a line between the eyebrow and headline that names the arrival:**

```
EYEBROW     THE STANDARD FOR SRI LANKAN E-COMMERCE

NEW LINE    You are in the right place.

HEADLINE    From seller to
            Entrepreneur.
```

This three-word addition — *"You are in the right place."* — does something no visual treatment can do. It speaks directly to the seller's state of mind when they land. They arrived uncertain. These four words resolve that uncertainty before the headline even lands.

That resolution — that feeling of having found what you were looking for — is the premium feeling you are building toward.
