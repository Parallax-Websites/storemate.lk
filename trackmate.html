How it works

Three steps to
know exactly where your package is

Trackmate works with every major Sri Lankan courier. One app. Any package. Any courier.

01
Enter your tracking number

Type or paste your tracking number into Trackmate. Works with TransEx, Royal Express, Curfox, FDE, Citypak, DomEx, Koombiyo and more Sri Lankan couriers.

See real-time status

Trackmate checks the courier's live system and shows you the current status, In Transit, Out for Delivery, or Delivered, with the estimated delivery date.
