How it works

What is the
Self-Order Form?

It is one link, just for your shop. Your customer opens it, picks what they want, types their name and address, and clicks save. The order comes straight to you. You do nothing.

  • You share your link on ads, your page, or your bio.
  • Your customer picks the item and how many they want.
  • They type their name and address, then click save.
  • The order shows up in Storemate. You typed nothing.

The fix

Too many unknown chats can
get your number banned.

WhatsApp bans numbers that get too many messages from people they don't know. It looks like spam, and it breaks WhatsApp's rules. The Self-Order Form stops that from ever happening.

  • Customers order from your link, so they never message your number.
  • No pile of unknown chats, so nothing looks like spam.
  • Put your link on your ads and reach thousands, with no ban risk.
  • No risky bulk messages or broadcasts that get numbers blocked.
  • Even if your number is ever banned, your orders stay safe in Storemate.
