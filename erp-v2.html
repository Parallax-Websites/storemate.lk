ERP Products

Seven modules. One set of books.

Online-first means what other ERPs treat as add-ons sits in the core here: courier sync, COD reconciliation, return risk and one stock figure across every channel. Pick the module closest to the problem you have this month.

Module 01 of 7

Every order, every channel, one queue.

The module Storemate started with, and the reason online sellers switch. Orders stop living in chat threads and notebooks.

Orders in one place

  • WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, phone and website orders in a single list
  • Self-order forms customers fill through a link you share
  • Duplicate order detection across every channel
  • Full customer and return history on every order line

Courier work, automated

  • One click syncs to Royal Express, TransEx, FDE, DomEx and Koombiyo
  • Live status on every shipment: in progress, successful, failed
  • Branded waybills in 30 seconds, not 10 minutes
  • Bulk waybill printing with duplicate print prevention

COD, reconciled

  • COD collections matched against courier remittances
  • Fake and repeat-return customers flagged before you ship
  • Return rate typically down from around 20% to under 5%
  • Delivery cost carried against each order, not averaged

Real stock control

More than one outlet

  • Per-branch stock with transfers between outlets

  • Group and per-outlet reporting side by side

Module 03 of 7

Payroll that already knows the Sri Lankan rules.

The gap every foreign ERP leaves open. Attendance, leave and payroll running inside the same system as your sales.

Statutory, built in

  • EPF at 12% employer and 8% employee
  • ETF at 3% employer
  • APIT withheld against the current IRD tables
  • Submission files generated for monthly filing

Attendance and leave

  • Attendance, shifts, overtime and no-pay feeding payroll directly
  • Leave entitlement and carry-forward under the Shop and Office Act
  • Approval flows for leave and overtime claims
  • Multi-outlet rosters for businesses with more than one location

Payroll runs

  • Payslips generated and shared digitally
  • Allowances, deductions and advances per employee
  • Bank transfer files for salary payment
  • Labour cost carried through to jobs and production

Module 05 of 7

From recipe to finished goods, costed as it moves.

For online businesses that make what they sell, whether that is cosmetics, food, garments or assembled goods.

Recipes and BOM

  • Multi-level bill of materials with live cost calculation
  • Wastage allowances built into each recipe
  • Labour operations costed per step
  • Overhead percentage and gross margin against selling price

Production runs

  • Job orders raised against a recipe
  • Stage-by-stage tracking, line by line
  • Material issue and return against each batch
  • Wastage recorded where it actually happens

Quality and output

  • Finishing and QC checkpoints before stock becomes sellable
  • Rejects and rework tracked separately from wastage
  • Batch and expiry carried into finished goods

Module 06 of 7

Know which jobs made money before the month closes.

For repairs, installations, servicing and any work quoted per job rather than sold per unit. Formerly Storemate Job Costing.

Jobs and tickets

  • Service jobs raised from a customer request or a sold item
  • Checklists and stage sign-off per job type
  • Technician assignment with a schedule view
  • Job status visible to the customer on request

Parts and labour

  • Stock requests and approval flow against each job
  • Parts issued from inventory, not guessed at afterwards

Margin and warranty

  • Quoted versus actual margin per job and per customer

Module 07 of 7

Every asset, and who is holding it.

Laptops, delivery bikes, counter hardware, printers and software licences. The things that quietly go missing as a team grows.

The register

  • All assets in one list, with add and bulk import
  • Asset groups: fixed, non-fixed and software licence
  • Asset types split into digital and non-digital
  • Categories, brands and your own custom fields

Who has what

Upkeep and control

  • Settings for numbering, categories and approval rules
  • Dashboard covering asset count, value and assignment status

Day 1 · free

How we start

A call or a visit to understand how the business runs today. How orders arrive, how many channels, who touches stock, where the month-end goes wrong. If Storemate ERP is not the right fit, this is where we say so.

You leave knowing which modules you need, in what order, and what it will cost.

Day 1–3

Your data, moved by us

Bring a spreadsheet, an old system export, or the file your accountant keeps. We have worked from all three. Items, customers, suppliers, stock on hand and opening balances go in.

We reconcile the opening figures with you before anything switches on, because an ERP that starts from wrong balances is worse than the spreadsheet it replaced.

Day 3–5

Configured with your accountant in the room

Modules switched on, roles and approval limits set, tax configured, and your chart of accounts built to match how you already report. Not a template chart you have to work around.

This is the step most implementations rush. We do not, because everything downstream depends on it.

Week 2

We train your staff, not just you

Hands-on sessions on your own data, in Sinhala, Tamil or English. Counter staff learn billing and returns, warehouse learns receiving and transfers, office learns approvals and reports.

On site if you want us there, screen-share if that suits your team better and saves waiting for a visit.

Week 3–6

Handover happens after your books close, not before

Going live is the easy part. The test is your first month-end inside the system. We stay close through it, check the figures with you, and fix what surfaces.

Then you have a named person to call, not a ticket queue.
