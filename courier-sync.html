FAQ

Common questions about courier sync

Which couriers does Storemate sync with?
Storemate currently syncs with Royal Express, FDE Express, DomEx, TransEx, and Koombiyo, and we're adding more regularly. If your courier isn't on the list, contact our team and we'll check if an integration is possible.
Do I need to set up the courier connection myself?
No. Our team handles the full setup. When you sign up, we connect your Storemate account to your courier partner and make sure your first sync goes through correctly. You don't need to deal with API keys, portal settings, or any technical configuration.
What happens if a sync fails?
You'll see it immediately on your sync dashboard, failed orders are flagged the moment the sync attempt fails, not hours later. One click re-syncs the order. Nothing is lost silently and no order slips through to the customer as unshipped.
Can I use multiple couriers for different orders?
Yes. You choose which courier to sync each order to, or split orders across multiple couriers from a single screen. You can also switch couriers at the last moment without re-entering any data, Storemate transfers everything to the new courier automatically.
How quickly does an order sync to the courier?
Syncs happen in seconds. The moment you confirm the sync, the order data is sent directly to the courier's system, no batch delays, no scheduled uploads. The courier receives the full order immediately.
Do I still need to log in to the courier portal after syncing?
No. Once the sync is confirmed, the courier has everything they need. Your team manages all orders from Storemate, there's no reason to log in to a separate courier portal. The sync dashboard gives you full visibility of what was sent.
What information gets sent to the courier when an order syncs?
Everything the courier needs, customer name, delivery address, phone number, product description, COD amount, special handling notes, and any packaging instructions. Nothing needs to be re-entered or filled in manually on the courier side.

Can't connect your courier partner?

If you run into trouble connecting your courier or your courier isn't listed, our support team will handle it directly. Just reach out, we'll get the integration set up for you.
