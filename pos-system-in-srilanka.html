Live inventory

Inventory that manages itself

From a single shelf to multiple warehouses, Storemate keeps stock, purchases and suppliers in one place, and keeps every number current without a manual count.

  • Real time stock control See what's in stock, by outlet, right now

Bring a spreadsheet, a printed price list, or the notebook under the counter. We've worked from all three. Barcodes, prices, categories, variants, units and opening stock go in, and we check them with you before you bill a single customer.

Questions

POS & inventory, answered

Including the awkward ones. If yours isn't here, ask us and we'll add it.

What is the best POS system in Sri Lanka?

The best POS system in Sri Lanka for retail and wholesale businesses combines fast barcode billing with real time inventory control. Storemate POS is built for Sri Lanka sellers. It runs in any browser on Windows PCs or Android tablets, supports Sinhala and English receipts, and connects counter sales with online orders in one system. It is free to start with no monthly contract.

What is Storemate POS?

Storemate POS is a point of sale built right into Storemate OMS. It lets you bill customers with barcode scanning, print or share receipts, and keeps your stock, sales and profit updated automatically, all inside the same system you use to manage orders.

Does stock update automatically when I sell?

Yes. The moment a bill is saved, stock is reduced in real time across every outlet and device, so you never oversell and never rely on end of day manual counts. Returns and stock transfers update the same way.

Do I need special hardware?

No special hardware is required. Storemate POS runs in a web browser on any Windows PC, laptop or Android device. You can add a barcode scanner and a thermal or A4 receipt printer if you want them, but they are optional.

Is the POS a separate product?

No. It is a point of sale module built into Storemate OMS, not a separate app to buy or log into. Your counter sales, online orders, stock and customers all live in one place, so there is nothing extra to reconcile.

Can I track suppliers and purchases?

Yes. You can record purchases, receive goods into stock, and track balances owed to each supplier, all connected to your live inventory so your buying and stock stay in sync.

Does it connect with my online orders?

Yes. Because the POS is part of Storemate OMS, your in store sales and online orders share the same products, stock and customers automatically, so you can sell at the counter and online without anything going out of sync.

Does it support Sinhala and Tamil?

Receipts print in Sinhala and English. Our team supports you in Sinhala, Tamil and English by phone, WhatsApp and on site.

What happens to my data if I stop using it?

Your business records are yours. Email hello@storemate.lk and we will provide a full export of your products, sales, customers and stock in a usable format within 14 days.
