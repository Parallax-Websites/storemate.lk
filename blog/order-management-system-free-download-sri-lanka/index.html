01

What "Free Order Management System" Usually Means

When people search for a free OMS download, they generally find one of three things:

1A free mobile app with a free tier

Some order management platforms offer a mobile app (Android/iOS) that's free to download and use up to a certain order volume, after which you upgrade to a paid plan.

2A free trial

Full access to the software for a limited time (7, 14, or 30 days), after which a subscription is required to continue.

3A "freemium" desktop or web tool

Basic order tracking features are free, while advanced features (courier integration, COD reconciliation, reporting) are locked behind a paid plan.

Very few order management systems are permanently 100% free with no limitations, and that's usually intentional, since running the servers, courier integrations, and support behind an OMS has real ongoing costs.

02

What to Check Before You Download Any "Free" OMS

Before installing anything, especially from outside an official app store, check the following:

Is it from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store? Official app stores screen apps for basic safety. Avoid downloading OMS software as a raw APK file from a random website. This is a common way pirated or modified software carries malware.

Does the "free" version actually cover your needs? Check order volume limits, number of connected sales channels, and whether COD reconciliation is included. This is often the first feature locked behind a paid tier.

Who has access to your data? Since an OMS handles customer names, phone numbers, and addresses, only use software from a provider with a clear privacy policy and Sri Lankan business presence you can actually contact if something goes wrong.

Is there a catch on migration? Some "free" tools make it easy to get your order data in, but charge or restrict exporting your data back out later.

03

Features You Should Expect From a Free Plan

A reasonable free tier for order management software in Sri Lanka should typically include:

  • Basic order capture from at least one sales channel
  • Manual or limited inventory tracking
  • A capped number of monthly orders (commonly somewhere between 50–200)
  • Basic order status tracking (pending, dispatched, delivered)

Features that are usually reserved for paid plans include multi-channel order syncing, automated courier integration, COD reconciliation, and detailed reporting: the features that matter most once your order volume grows.

04

Free Plan vs. Paid Plan: What Changes

What typically changes between a free and paid order management plan.

Free Plan

  • Single-channel order capture
  • Manual inventory tracking
  • Capped monthly orders (~50–200)
  • Basic status tracking
  • No COD reconciliation
  • No advanced reports

Paid Plan

  • Multi-channel sync (Daraz, FB, IG, website)
  • Real-time inventory sync
  • Higher / unlimited order volume
  • Automated courier integration
  • Full COD payment reconciliation
  • Sales and return analytics dashboard

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a completely free order management system in Sri Lanka with no limitations?

Most legitimate providers offer a free tier or trial rather than an unlimited free version, since ongoing courier integrations and support have real costs. Be cautious of any tool claiming to be entirely free with no restrictions.

Is it safe to download order management software from outside the Play Store or App Store?

It's generally not recommended. APK files downloaded from unofficial websites are a common way for malware or spyware to be installed, especially concerning for software that handles customer data.

What happens to my data if I stop using the free plan?

This varies by provider. Always check whether you can export your order and customer data before you start relying on a "free" tool.

Can I upgrade later if I outgrow the free plan?

Yes. Most providers, including Storemate, allow you to upgrade to a paid plan as your order volume grows, without needing to migrate to different software.

Conclusion

A free order management system download is a sensible way to test whether centralized order management fits your business, as long as you download from an official source and understand exactly what the free tier includes.

Once your order volume grows past what a free plan supports, upgrading within the same platform, rather than switching tools entirely, keeps your order history and workflow intact.
