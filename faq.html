Getting Started

Storemate OMS is an order management system built for Sri Lankan online sellers. It brings all your orders, from Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram and phone, into one place, then helps you confirm them, sync to your courier, and print branded waybills. No more juggling spreadsheets and screenshots.

It's for anyone selling online in Sri Lanka and shipping by cash on delivery, whether you're handling your first few orders a day or thousands a month. From solo sellers to growing teams with multiple brands, Storemate scales with you.

No. You can start on our free plan with no card and no commitment. You only pay when you choose to upgrade for more orders, users or features.

Most sellers are up and running the same day. And you're not on your own, our team handles your full setup, including products, courier connections and team training, so you start with everything ready to go.

Orders & Couriers

An order is any customer purchase request that comes in through Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram or a phone call. In Storemate, each order is tracked from the moment it's placed all the way through to delivery.

Storemate connects with the major couriers across Sri Lanka, including Royal Express, TransEx, FDE, DomEx and Koombiyo. You can pick a courier per order. If your preferred partner isn't listed yet, contact us, we add new ones regularly.

COD (Cash on Delivery) Sync means that when you add orders to Storemate, the system automatically sends those details to your courier, no manual copying and pasting into courier portals every morning.

Yes. Every waybill prints with your logo and brand, filled in automatically from the order, about 30 seconds instead of 10 minutes. Built-in duplicate-print protection keeps things clean.
