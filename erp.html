The Disconnected Business Problem

Running your business across spreadsheets and silos

Sales in one app, stock in a sheet, purchasing on paper, accounts with someone else. Nothing talks to the next thing, so every number has to be found, checked and re-typed by hand.

5+ tools
Every department on a different system that does not talk to the next. The same order, product and payment gets entered again and again, and the numbers still never quite match.

Endless Data Re-Entry

The same order is typed into billing, then a stock sheet, then the accounts book. Every re-key is another chance for a costly mistake.

No Single Source Of Truth

Every tool shows a different figure and everyone trusts a different one. Decisions get made on data nobody is really sure about.

Slow Month-End Close

Closing the books means days of exporting, matching and correcting across tools, so the picture is always weeks out of date.

Blind Across Branches

Each outlet or warehouse keeps its own books and stock. You never see the whole business in one place, only fragments.

Reports Built By Hand

Every report is a manual export-and-merge job. By the time it is ready, the moment to act on it has already passed.

Stock & Cash Leaks

With no connected trail, shrinkage, unpaid dues and pricing errors slip through the gaps and quietly drain the business.

Questions

ERP, answered

What is Storemate ERP?
Storemate ERP is one connected platform that runs the core of your business, sales and POS, inventory, purchasing, finance and reporting, so a single entry flows everywhere it is needed. Instead of stitching together separate tools and spreadsheets, everything shares the same live data.
Do you help with implementation, or is it self-service?
Our team handles the whole rollout as a service: setting the system up around how you work, migrating your data, customizing where needed, and training your staff. You are never left to configure a complex ERP on your own.
Can you migrate our data from spreadsheets or our old system?
Yes. We bring across your products, customers, suppliers and opening stock from spreadsheets or your existing system, clean it up, and load it so you start with accurate data on day one.
Can the ERP be customized to our business?
Yes. Beyond the standard modules, we tailor screens, reports and workflow rules to your industry and the way you actually operate, so the system fits your business rather than forcing you to change everything.
Can I run multiple outlets and warehouses?
Yes. You manage every outlet and warehouse from one login, move stock between them with transfers, and see the whole company in a single view while still tracking each location on its own.
Do I need to buy everything at once?
No. You can start with the modules you need today, such as sales, POS and inventory, and switch on purchasing, finance and more as you grow, all inside the same system so you never have to migrate again.
