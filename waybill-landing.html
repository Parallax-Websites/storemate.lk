The Waybill Problem

What your waybills say
about your business

The waybill is the first physical thing your customer sees — before they open the box, before they see the product. It either builds trust or destroys it.

10 min
Wasted per order on waybill handling. Writing by hand. Selecting templates manually. Printing the same order twice. Hours lost every day — and your brand invisible throughout.

Manual Handwritten Waybills

Order arrives. Staff member handwrites the waybill. Messy. Unprofessional. Customer sees it and loses confidence before they even open the package.

Generic Courier Branding

You print default courier waybills. Customer sees the courier's logo, not yours. Your business becomes invisible on every delivery. Missed brand reinforcement on every package.

Time Wasting

Manual handwriting takes 5–10 minutes per waybill. Generic printing requires manual template selection. When you ship 50 orders a day, that's hours lost every single day.

Duplicate Printing

No tracking of what has been printed. Staff prints the same order twice. Wasted paper. Wasted labels. Confusion at dispatch. Orders shipped with wrong waybills attached.

Zero Customization

Can't customize waybills for different brands or couriers. No seasonal designs. No special occasion formats. Same boring template for every single order — no matter what.

Wrong Data On Waybills

Manual handwriting leads to wrong details. Wrong address. Wrong item code. Courier confused. Package delayed or sent to the wrong location. Customer furious.
